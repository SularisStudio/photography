About Sularis Studios

Storytelling through light, emotion, and real moments

Our Story

Sularis Studios LLC is a family owned company offering professional photography services across the Twin Cities metro area and the greater Brainerd Lakes region, including Pequot Lakes and surrounding communities.

At Sularis Studios, photography is more than just images — it’s about capturing the feeling behind the moment. Whether it's a wedding, senior portrait, or family session, our goal is to create timeless visuals that feel authentic and personal.

We focus on natural light, real emotion, and a cinematic style that turns everyday moments into lasting memories. Every session is designed to feel relaxed, comfortable, and true to you.

Based on passion and creativity, Sularis Studios is built on the idea that the best photos come from genuine connection.
